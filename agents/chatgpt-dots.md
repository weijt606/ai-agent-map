One-line take: a dot is OpenAI's always-on agent inside ChatGPT — one per person, powered by GPT-6 Astra, with its own cloud computer and browser — that holds an ongoing responsibility, decides when to follow up, reaches you in ChatGPT, Slack, Teams or a call, and hands concrete jobs to ChatGPT Work or [Codex](codex.md).

## How It Differs From The Rest Of ChatGPT

OpenAI draws the line itself: **Chat** answers, **ChatGPT Work** takes a delegated task now or on a schedule, **Codex** builds software, and **your dot** keeps track of a responsibility over time, works out next steps as things change, and follows up between conversations. A dot can start Work or Codex tasks, use background agents in parallel, and wake itself up later — "you don't need a fixed schedule for every follow-up".

## When To Pick It

- You have an **ongoing responsibility** (an event, a project, a renewal) rather than a one-off task, and you want something that notices what changed and brings you decisions.
- You live in Slack or Teams: it is the same dot, with the same memory, wherever you talk to it.
- You already pay for Pro and want the ceiling model behind your agent.

## When Not To Pick It

- You are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, or not on Pro.
- You want a cheap agent: personal access starts at $100 a month.
- You want a narrowly scoped tool. A dot draws on your ChatGPT memory, its own private notes and every enabled plugin; that breadth is the product.

## Permissions And Data

Before an action affects your accounts or shares information, an automatic review decides whether the dot can proceed, needs your approval, or must hand the step to you; your own instructions and custom rules add limits but "neither instructions nor custom rules override built-in safety requirements". Logins go through a private sign-in form, not the conversation, and you can **Take over** its cloud browser at any time. Its background research is read-only; follow-up actions need the applicable permissions. The docs this map read do not state training terms specific to dots.

## Bottom Line

The most ambitious of the big-three assistants' agents — ceiling model, own computer, multi-channel — and the most expensive of the three, closed to Europe. If you are on Pro outside Europe, it is the one to evaluate first; if not, it is not yet a choice you can make.
