## What The Agent Does

From the app's own description:

- **Work assistant (工作助理)** — takes a complex task and delivers finished files: web pages, apps, PPT, Word, Excel, images.
- **Scheduled tasks (定时任务)** — recurring or one-off, triggered at the set time and delivered automatically.
- **Life helper (AI生活帮手)** — food delivery, hotel booking, restaurant search, government services, shopping advice.
- **Agent plaza (智能体广场)** — official brand agents you call with an @ in the conversation.

**Qwen Go! (千问Go!)**, a personal agent for errands such as claims and refunds, was reported by Chinese media on Oct 13 2026 as a waitlist-only feature inside the app. This map has not found a first-party page for it and records it as announced, not shipped.

## When To Pick It

- You live in China and want an agent that **closes errands inside the services you already use**.
- You want finished office files rather than chat answers, on a schedule.

## When Not To Pick It

- You are outside China: the errand features depend on Chinese services.
- You need written terms on what the agent may do with your accounts and payments; the listing describes features, not permissions.

## Bottom Line

A broad "agent does real errands" pitch, with the deliverables and scheduling to match. Its value is the ecosystem it sits in — which is also the limit on who can use it.
