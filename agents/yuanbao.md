One-line take: Yuanbao is Tencent's free consumer assistant, and since July 2026 it has an **Agent feature on Tencent's Hy3 model** that executes a task and hands back a finished PPT, Word, Excel, PDF or HTML file — all free — making it the zero-cost entry point to the same model family behind [WorkBuddy](workbuddy.md).

## Where It Sits

Tencent runs one model across several surfaces: Hy3 powers Yuanbao, [WorkBuddy](workbuddy.md) (the desktop office agent this map already profiles), CodeBuddy and Marvis. Yuanbao is the **chat-first, deliverable-producing** end; WorkBuddy is the desktop end that works on your files. This map found **no first-party evidence** that Yuanbao operates other apps, controls a phone or runs scheduled tasks.

## When To Pick It

- You want office deliverables from a request, **for free**, in Chinese.
- You already use Tencent's ecosystem and want the consumer end of the Hy3 family before trying WorkBuddy.

## When Not To Pick It

- You want errands, cross-app actions or scheduling: those are not documented for Yuanbao.
- You are outside China.

## Bottom Line

The free baseline of this list: no subscription, real deliverables, and the narrowest documented agent scope. Use it to see what Hy3 does; move to [WorkBuddy](workbuddy.md) when the work needs your files.
